About Our Masjid

Nurturing Faith, Knowledge, and Service Since 1992

Our Foundation & Mission

Established in 1992 (1412 AH) under the supervision of local Islamic scholars, Al-Noor Grand Masjid & Islamic Center has blossomed from a humble storefront prayer room to a sprawling, majestic multi-wing complex serving thousands of Muslim families weekly.

We operate on the balanced principles of the Quran and authentic Prophetic Sunnah, stressing moderate understanding, absolute moral excellence, scientific awareness, and constructive contribution to the wider modern human community.

Core Services Available

  • ✔ Daily Congregational Prayers
  • ✔ Islamic Marriage Contracts (Nikah)
  • ✔ Weekend Quranic Academy
  • ✔ Funeral & Bereavement Support
  • ✔ Financial Zakat Distribution
  • ✔ Interfaith Seminars
